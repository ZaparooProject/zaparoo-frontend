The files from AliceMC10 through Sega32X.jp were converted to grayscale or
single-color SVGs. All bundled files were optimized for size and are tinted
to the theme at runtime; other changes are noted per file.

Trademark notice: the logo and platform names may be protected as trademarks
in some jurisdictions. They are included on a nominative-use basis to identify
the platform whose content the frontend organizes; no endorsement or
affiliation is implied or claimed.
