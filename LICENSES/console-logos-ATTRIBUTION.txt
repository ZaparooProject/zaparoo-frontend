Console Logos Attribution
=========================

This software bundles a curated subset of platform logos under
resources/images/systems/ derived from:

  Console Logos Professionally Redrawn + Official Versions
  by Dan Patrick
  https://archive.org/details/console-logos-professionally-redrawn-plus-official-versions

The bundled system SVGs are optimized copies from the "Dark - Black & White"
SVG set in the GitHub mirror, tinted at runtime by the frontend. Optional
full-color system PNGs are restored from earlier bundled assets and regional
"Dark - Color" variants from the same mirror:

  https://github.com/PRO100BYTE/console-logos
  Mirror copyright (c) 2023 #TheDayG0ne and PRO100BYTE Team
  Licensed under the MIT License.

Later additions take both the black-and-white SVG and, where the set has
one, the matching "Dark - Color" PNG. Some systems show a maker's mark from
the set because no mark of their own exists: the Irem, Jaleco, Namco and
Taito marks for those makers' arcade boards, Acorn for the Atom, and
"PC Games" for PC. Modified files:

  resources/images/systems/CBMII.svg         Commodore mark cropped from
                                             the Commodore PET logo
  resources/images/systems/QL.svg            Sinclair mark cropped from the
                                             Sinclair ZX81 logo
  resources/images/systems/SegaSystem18.svg  Sega System 16 logo with the
                                             "6" redrawn as an "8"
  resources/images/systems/NESMusic.svg,
  resources/images/systems/SNESMusic.svg     the bundled NES and SNES logos
                                             with a Lucide music note added
