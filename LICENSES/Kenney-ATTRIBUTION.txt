Kenney Input Prompts Attribution
================================

This software bundles the help-bar button and D-pad glyphs under
resources/images/buttons/{style_a,style_b,style_c,style_d,style_e}/ (face
buttons, shoulders, and D-pad/arrow-key glyphs for every style), derived
from:

  Input Prompts (1.5)
